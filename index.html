Office tour souvenir

A little reminder of your visit.

MEPS Tour Display is a desktop kiosk that walks visitors through an office tour and creates a souvenir PDF. Visitors can request a copy by entering their email address at the display.

Your souvenir, by email

When email delivery is enabled, the display sends the requested PDF as an attachment from meps.am.tour@gmail.com.

Visitors do not sign in with Google. Enter an address where you would like to receive your souvenir.

Connected by a guide

A guide connects the designated Gmail account. The display uses Google's Gmail API to send souvenirs and verify the sender's email identity.

The integration does not read the Gmail inbox. See the Gmail integration privacy notice for access, storage, and delivery details.

Contact the display team

For a question about the display or an emailed souvenir, contact meps.am.tour@gmail.com.
