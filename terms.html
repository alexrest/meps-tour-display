Souvenir email service

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Requesting a souvenir

The email feature is for visitors requesting a copy of their own tour souvenir. Use an email address you control or have permission to use, and check it before submitting. This feature is not a mailing-list subscription.

The display does not charge visitors to request a souvenir email. Delivery depends on the kiosk's connection, its guide's delivery settings, Google, and the recipient's email provider. Receipt, timing, and inbox placement are not guaranteed.

Account connection and privacy

Only an authorized guide should connect the designated sender account and manage delivery. Visitors do not need a Google account to request a souvenir.

Read the Gmail integration privacy notice for information about account access and stored souvenir records. Contact meps.am.tour@gmail.com for help with a request.
